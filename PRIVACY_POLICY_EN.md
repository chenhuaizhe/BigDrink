# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 13, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to BigDrink (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "the app"). We highly value your privacy and the protection of your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## Information Collection and Use

### HealthKit Data
- Our app uses the Apple HealthKit framework to record and read your water intake data.
- We only collect data related to water intake (dietaryWater).
- This data is used for:
  - Recording your daily water intake
  - Displaying your water intake history
  - Generating water consumption statistics and analysis

### Data Storage
- All HealthKit-related data is stored in Apple's Health app
- The app uses Apple's CloudKit service to store in iCloud:
  - Water intake records
  - Personal settings and preferences
- The app stores locally:
  - Local copies of water intake records
  - App settings and preferences
  - Temporary cache data
- iCloud sync enables data synchronization across your devices
- You can control iCloud sync in your iPhone's system settings
- We do not upload your data to any servers other than Apple's

## Data Sharing

We commit to:
- Never selling your HealthKit data to third parties
- Never using your health data for advertising or marketing purposes
- Never sharing your data with third parties without your explicit permission

## Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:
- Only accessing HealthKit data after receiving your explicit authorization
- Strictly adhering to Apple's privacy guidelines and HealthKit usage regulations
- Using standard system security mechanisms to protect your data

## Notifications

Our app sends water intake reminder notifications:
- These notifications are entirely based on local settings and do not collect or transmit any personal data
- You can manage notification preferences in the app settings at any time

## Your Rights

You have the following rights:
- View, modify, or delete your health data in the iPhone's "Health" app at any time
- Revoke HealthKit access permissions in system settings
- Manage app notification settings

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not targeted at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through in-app notifications or other appropriate means.

## Contact Us

For any questions, suggestions, or feedback, please use the in-app feedback feature in BigDrink's settings. We value your input and will respond as soon as possible.

## Compliance Statement

This app complies with:
- Apple's App Store Review Guidelines
- HealthKit Framework Usage Guidelines
- Relevant Data Protection Laws and Regulations
